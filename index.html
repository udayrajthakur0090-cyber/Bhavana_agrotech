ABOUT BHAWANA BIOTECH

Helping farms grow
with purpose.

Bhawana Biotech Pvt. Ltd. is an agricultural technology and consultancy firm headquartered in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh.

We work to modernize Indian agriculture through scientific, sustainable and commercially focused farming practices. Our goal is to help farmers, landowners and investors plan agricultural ventures with better preparation, technical guidance and crop management.
